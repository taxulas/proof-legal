Privacy Policy

Proof is built so that your photos stay on your iPhone.

Last updated: 2026-09-06

In short. Proof stores your photos and your progress in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It does not require an account. It does not upload your photos to a Proof server. All image analysis runs on your device. There is no advertising and no advertising tracking. You can delete everything from inside the app.

Who is responsible

Proof is an independent iPhone app. For any question about this policy or about your data, write to proofapp.contact@gmail.com.

What Proof processes, and where it is stored

Everything below is stored locally in the app's private storage area on your iPhone, protected by iOS app sandboxing and by your device encryption.

  • Your photos. Photos you take with the in-app camera or choose from your photo library are copied into a private folder belonging to the app. They are not written back to your photo library and they are not sent anywhere.
  • Analysis results. When Proof compares two photos, it computes geometric information — silhouette outlines, body-pose points, framing, and comparability scores. These results are numbers describing shape and alignment. They are stored next to your photos on the device.
  • Optional masked copies. If you turn on face masking, Proof creates a masked copy of the photo on your device and uses that copy in the app and in anything you export.
  • Your settings and progress. Your goal, weekly sessions, chosen language, reminder setting, comparison history, and any measurement you enter yourself.
  • Product usage events. Proof records short technical events such as "app opened", "photo selected" or "analysis completed", together with small non-identifying values such as a count or a score. These are kept in a local queue on the device, limited to the most recent 250 entries. In the current release they are not transmitted anywhere — see "Components included but not active" below.

What Proof does not do

  • It does not require or offer a Proof account, and asks for no name, email or phone number.
  • It does not upload your photos to a Proof server. Proof operates no server that receives your photos.
  • It does not show advertising and does not use advertising identifiers.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not build advertising profiles.
  • It does not sell or rent personal data.

Analysis happens on your device

Proof uses Apple's Vision framework, which is part of iOS, to detect faces, body-pose points and silhouettes in your photos. This processing runs locally on your iPhone. Your photos are not sent to Apple by Proof for this analysis, and they are not sent to us.

Face masking

Face masking is optional and off unless you enable it. When enabled, Proof detects faces on the device and stores a masked copy. The behaviour is deliberately fail-closed: if a masked copy cannot be produced for a photo, that photo is hidden rather than shown unmasked.

Sharing is always your choice

Proof only shares an image when you explicitly ask it to. It then hands the file to the standard iOS share sheet, and you choose the destination. Once you send an image to another app or service, that app's own terms and privacy policy apply to it.

Notifications

If you enable the weekly reminder, Proof schedules a local notification on your device. It uses no push server, and no notification content leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

Any subscription or purchase offered in Proof is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes the payment and the billing relationship. Proof never sees or stores your payment card, and receives no payment details from Apple. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Components included but not active

We think it is fairer to tell you what is inside the app than to describe only what runs. The app bundle currently contains two third-party components that are not configured and not active in the released version, because no credentials for them are compiled into the build:

  • A purchase SDK (RevenueCat). Included to support subscriptions, but not initialised in the current release. It therefore processes no data and makes no network request. If it is activated in a future version, it would receive purchase and device-level technical data needed to validate a subscription — never your photos.
  • A product-analytics integration (PostHog). Present in the code but disabled, so usage events stay in the local queue described above and no analytics request is sent. If it is ever enabled, it would receive only the product usage events described above under a random identifier generated on the device — never your photos and never the analysis of them.

This page will be updated before any such component becomes active in a released version.

Retention

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. Proof applies no server-side retention period, because it holds nothing on a server. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes the app's private storage, including your photos and analyses.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything from inside the app, in Settings, using the option to delete all data. This removes the stored app state and deletes the entire private photo folder, returning the app to the state of a fresh install. The only thing kept is the interface language you selected, which is a preference and not personal data.

Security

Your data is held in the app's sandboxed storage, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by the encryption iOS applies to your device. Keeping a passcode or Face ID enabled on your iPhone, and keeping iOS up to date, is the most effective protection for it. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, your Proof data may be included in that backup, which is then governed by the settings and terms of that backup service.

Children

Proof is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, data protection law gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability.

Because Proof keeps your photos, analyses and settings only on your own device and transmits none of it to us, we hold no copy of your data and have no way to identify you or to retrieve, export or delete your information on your behalf. In practice this means you exercise these rights directly and immediately in the app: your data is visible to you in the app, you can correct it by replacing or removing photos, and you can erase all of it with the delete option in Settings. If you believe something in this policy is inaccurate, or you want to ask a question about it, contact proofapp.contact@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes affecting what leaves your device will be reflected here before the version that makes the change is released.
