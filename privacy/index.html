Privacy Policy

Proof keeps your photos private and processes images on your iPhone.

Last updated: 2026-09-16

In short. Proof does not require an account. Photos are copied to the app's private storage and image analysis runs on your device. Proof does not upload your photos to its own server. Subscription information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat. Sharing and data export only happen when you ask for them.

Who is responsible

Proof is an independent iPhone app. For any question about this policy or your data, write to proofapp.contact@gmail.com.

Data stored on your iPhone

  • Photos. Photos you take in Proof or select with the iOS photo picker are copied into Proof's private app storage. Proof preserves the original file and also creates the local copies it needs for display and analysis.
  • On-device analysis. Apple Vision runs locally to assess pose, framing, visibility and comparability. Proof stores the resulting technical values with your local app data. Proof does not send your photos to Apple or to a Proof server for this analysis.
  • Face-masked copies. Face masking is on by default. The original is preserved; Proof creates a separate masked copy. When masking is selected, a missing masked copy is never replaced on screen or in a media share by the unmasked image.
  • App data. Your timeline, comparisons, settings, reminders and other progress information are stored locally in the app's private storage.
  • Local usage queue. Proof records a limited queue of product events such as opening the app or completing an import. The queue contains no photo, file path, exact capture date, pose point or body-zone value and is limited to 250 events.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. Proof does not receive your payment-card details. Proof uses RevenueCat to load subscription offerings, validate App Store purchase receipts, manage the Proof Pro entitlement, restore purchases and understand subscription performance.

For those purposes, purchase history and subscription status, an automatically generated RevenueCat app-user identifier, and limited app, device and Store technical information are sent to RevenueCat. Proof does not send photos or image-analysis results to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Product analytics

The limited product events described above are always written to the local queue. The app sends them to PostHog only when PostHog credentials are included in the distributed build. When enabled, Proof uses a random identifier generated on the device; it does not send photos, file paths, exact capture dates, pose points or body-zone values. This analytics is not used for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites. See PostHog's Privacy Policy.

Photo picker and camera

On iOS, Proof uses the system photo picker so you choose which images to provide. Camera access is requested only when you choose the camera. The selected or captured file is then copied into Proof's private storage.

Sharing and exports

Proof only opens the iOS share sheet when you ask it to. You choose the receiving app or service, whose own terms and privacy practices then apply.

Media sharing uses the masked copy when face masking is on. Export My Data is different: its ZIP archive is generated locally and contains your unmasked original photos, an inventory and a README. It is not a complete one-click backup and cannot automatically restore all Proof state on another iPhone. Once you save or send a copy outside Proof, Proof cannot control or delete that copy.

Notifications

If you enable the weekly reminder, Proof asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your iPhone. Proof uses no push-notification server.

Retention, deletion and backups

Your photos and app state remain in Proof's private storage until you remove them or delete the app. iCloud or computer backups may include app data according to your device and backup settings.

Delete My Data cancels Proof's local reminder and removes the local app state, private photo folder, Replay and export caches, the prior local analytics queue and its random analytics identifier. A new local deletion event may then be recorded. Proof keeps your selected interface language and a non-personal installation marker used to avoid counting the same installation as new twice.

Delete My Data cannot remove files you already exported or shared, copies held in device or cloud backups, or purchase and transaction records held by Apple or RevenueCat. It does not cancel an active App Store subscription. Restore Purchases restores subscription access only; it does not restore your photos, timeline or settings.

Security

Proof uses the iOS app sandbox and device encryption. Keep a device passcode or Face ID enabled and keep iOS up to date. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Children and medical use

Proof is intended for adults and is not directed at children. Proof is a visual progress tool, not a medical device, diagnosis or medical, nutritional or fitness advice.

Your choices and rights

You can inspect, replace, export and delete the data stored locally by Proof. Because that local data is not uploaded to Proof, support cannot retrieve it for you. For questions or requests about subscription or analytics information processed through Proof's providers, contact proofapp.contact@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability and the right to complain to your data-protection authority.
