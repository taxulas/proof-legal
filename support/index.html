Proof Support

Help with the app, and how to reach a human.

Contact

Write to proofapp.contact@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Proof version and the steps that caused the problem. Do not attach personal photos: they are not needed for support and should stay on your device.

A photo will not import

On iOS, Proof uses Apple's system photo picker: you choose the files made available to the app. Select one photo after I’m starting today, or between 2 and 100 after I already have progress photos. If an image is stored only in iCloud, open it in Photos and wait for it to download before trying again. A damaged or unsupported file may still be rejected.

The camera does not open

Proof requests camera access only when you choose the camera. If access was declined, enable it in Settings › Apps › Proof › Camera, then reopen Proof.

A comparison or analysis is unavailable

Proof does not produce a result when the photos are not comparable enough. Try again with the same angle and distance, a clearly visible full body, steady framing and even lighting. This protects the comparison from a misleading result.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstalled the app or changed device, open the subscription screen in the app and use the restore option with the same Apple account that made the purchase. Restore Purchases restores subscription access only; it does not restore photos, your timeline or settings. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account's subscription settings. Deleting Proof does not cancel it.

Exporting my data

Use Export my data in the app's You section. Proof creates a ZIP locally with your unmasked original photos, an inventory and a README. Store it carefully. The ZIP is not a complete backup and cannot automatically restore Proof on another iPhone.

Deleting my local data

Use Delete my data in the app's You section. It removes Proof's local state and private photo folder and cannot be undone. It cannot remove exported or shared files, copies in device or cloud backups, or purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat. It does not cancel a subscription.

Privacy

Proof does not require an account. Photos stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone and image analysis runs on the device. Proof is not a medical device or medical advice. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a bug

Send what happened, what you expected and the steps to reproduce it, plus your iPhone model, iOS version, Proof version and any displayed error. If useful, attach only a cropped screenshot with no personal photo or information.
