Proof Support

Help with the app, and how to reach a human.

Contact

Write to proofapp.contact@gmail.com.

Proof is maintained by a small team, so replies are not immediate and we do not promise a fixed response time. To help us answer usefully, please tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version, and what you did just before the problem appeared. Please do not attach personal photos to a support email — we do not need them, and we would rather they stayed on your device.

A photo will not import

Proof needs permission to read the photo you choose. Check Settings › Apps › Proof › Photos on your iPhone. Some images stored only in iCloud have to be downloaded to the device first, so a photo that is still syncing can fail to import — open it once in the Photos app, then try again. Very unusual or corrupted image files can also be rejected; if a specific photo consistently fails while others work, tell us and we will look at it.

The camera does not open

Check Settings › Apps › Proof › Camera is allowed. If the permission was declined once, iOS will not ask again, and it has to be re-enabled there. Closing and reopening the app after changing the setting resolves most cases.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstalled the app or changed device, open the subscription screen in the app and use the restore option. Restoring works with the same Apple account that made the original purchase. To manage or cancel a subscription, use the subscription settings of your Apple account — deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Deleting all my data

Open Settings in the app and choose the option to delete all data. This erases the stored app state and the entire private photo folder, returning Proof to the state of a fresh install. It cannot be undone, and we cannot recover it for you afterwards, because none of it is ever sent to us. Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes its local data.

Privacy

Your photos stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone, and the image analysis runs on the device. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a bug

Send us what happened, what you expected instead, and the steps that lead to it, along with your iPhone model, iOS version and app version. A screenshot of the screen where it goes wrong helps a lot — please crop or omit anything you would rather not share.
